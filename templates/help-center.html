Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 2-4 business days. Express delivery is available for next-day delivery in select cities.
What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by the receipt.
Are my payments secure?
Yes, all payments are processed securely. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your personal and payment information.
Do your products have a warranty?
All VoltCart branded electronics come with a 12-month manufacturer's warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship.
